# Curriculum Module 1: CBT

A 45-minute CBT group for adults, built for telehealth and screen share.

- [Group screen](https://bnuckols13.github.io/curriculum-module-1-cbt/): a check-in that collects the group's own examples (something you've been avoiding, a challenge you've set, something hard), three interactive diagrams that use those examples (the five-part chain, the five-part model, and two paths through something hard), a discussion board, and takeaways.
- [Dialogue guide](https://bnuckols13.github.io/curriculum-module-1-cbt/dialogue-guide.html): the facilitator's sheet, with timing and questions.

Sources: Greenberger and Padesky, *Mind Over Mood* (2nd ed., 2016); J. S. Beck, *Cognitive Behavior Therapy: Basics and Beyond* (3rd ed., 2020); Craske et al., "Maximizing exposure therapy: An inhibitory learning approach," *Behaviour Research and Therapy* (2014); Thompson-Brenner et al., *The Renfrew Unified Treatment for Eating Disorders and Comorbidity* (2021); Padesky and Kennerley, *Dialogues for Discovery* (2023).

Nothing typed on the screen is saved.
