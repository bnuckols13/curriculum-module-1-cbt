Automatic thoughts

The first thought usually shows up on its own, fast, and feels like a fact. CBT calls it an automatic thought. It's often a guess or a prediction. Noticing it is the first skill.

Psychoeducation · 3

:20–:25Two ways it can go

    Exposure

    Exposure means practicing the second path on purpose, one small step at a time, with your treatment team. The distress doesn't always drop during the practice. What changes is what you learn: "That was hard, and I got through it."
