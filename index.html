:14–:19The five-part model

CBT looks at five parts of an experience. The situation is around us. The other four are inside us, and each one affects the others. Click a part.

Five parts
Click a part to see what it means
Thoughts, feelings, body, and behavior all happen inside a situation. A change in one part can change the others.
Automatic thoughts

The first thought usually shows up on its own, fast, and feels like a fact. CBT calls it an automatic thought. It's often a guess about what something means. Noticing it is the first skill.

Psychoeducation · 3

:19–:23How a pattern keeps itself going

Some behaviors bring relief right away and make the same thought stronger later. Step through it.

  1. Thought: "I'll say something awkward at the party."
  2. Feeling: nervous, with a knot in the stomach.
  3. Behavior: stay home.
  4. Relief: the nervous feeling drops right away.
  5. Later: the thought never got tested, so it shows up stronger next time.
Short term and long term

Relief now and a stronger thought later is one way patterns keep going. CBT looks for a small change anywhere in the loop.
